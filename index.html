Seer

A personal assistant that recognises you on your own camera, then reads out what actually matters — your mail, your deadlines, your projects, and the few messages worth interrupting you for.

Privacy policy Terms of use

What it does

Seer runs on a personal computer and an Android phone. When its camera recognises the owner returning to their desk after being away, it assembles a short spoken briefing and reads it aloud. Nobody else's face triggers it.

SourceWhat Seer reads
GmailUnread inbox mail — sender and subject only
College mailMail forwarded into the same Gmail account and tagged with a label
Google CalendarEvents and deadlines in the next few days
ProjectsA projects.json file the owner maintains by hand
WhatsAppNotifications the operating system already shows, filtered to the important ones

Which messages count as important

Seer will interrupt for a message from a family contact, anything in a watched group, or anything mentioning a test, assignment, exam, viva, placement or interview. Everything else is held back and summarised once in the evening, then never mentioned again.

How it is built

One Flutter codebase compiled for Windows and Android. Face recognition runs entirely on the device: ArcFace via a local Python helper on Windows, ML Kit and MobileFaceNet on Android. Speech uses the operating system's own text-to-speech.

There is no Seer server. The application talks only to Google's APIs, using the owner's own account, with read-only permissions.

Who it is for

Seer is a personal project built by and for a single user. It is not a commercial product, it is not distributed through any app store, and it is not accepting sign-ups. It is published here because Google requires an OAuth application to have a public home page.
