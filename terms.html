2. What Tacit does with your data

This is the point of the product, so it is specific rather than reassuring.

Everything Tacit measures stays on your computer. There is no Tacit server. Nothing is sent to us, and there is nothing we could send it to.

WhatWhyWhere it goes
Face geometry To recognise you sitting down A numeric template on your disk. Never an image.
Foreground window titles To tell you what you were working on Your disk
Mail, calendar, messages To brief you Your disk. Read-only access, revocable at any time.
What you say to it To answer Transcribed on your machine by a local model

Tacit recognises only you. It does not identify anyone else who passes the camera. Other people are counted, not named, and nothing about them is stored — the matching loop stops at the first confident result and no measurement outlives the frame it came from.

You can see everything Tacit holds, and delete any of it, inside the application. Uninstalling offers to destroy all of it.

3. Biometric consent and destruction

Tacit will not measure your face until you have given written consent inside the application, and it records that you did and when.

You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings. Face recognition stops immediately and the stored template is destroyed. Tacit keeps working; it simply waits for you to open it rather than noticing you arrive.

Face data is destroyed when you withdraw consent, when you delete it in the application, when you uninstall and choose to remove your data, or within three years of your last interaction with Tacit — whichever comes first.

Your biometric data is never sold, leased, traded or otherwise profited from, and is never disclosed to anyone.

That is not a policy we could quietly change later. We do not receive the data in the first place. The full retention and destruction schedule is at the biometric policy.

4. Face recognition is not security

Tacit's face matching is a convenience trigger. It compares similarity scores and can be fooled by a sufficiently similar face or by a photograph. It must not be used to protect anything, and it is not an authentication mechanism.

5. It speaks out loud

Tacit reads sender names, subject lines and message text aloud into whatever room it is in. Anyone present will hear them. Settings exist to suppress this; using them when you are not alone is your responsibility.

6. Accuracy

Briefings are assembled automatically and may be incomplete, delayed or wrong. Muted chats produce no notification and are invisible to Tacit. Do not rely on it as your only means of learning about anything that matters.
