Privacy policy

Seer is personal software run by one person on their own devices. There is no Seer server, no analytics, and no third party receives your data.

HomeTerms of use
The short version. Everything Seer reads stays on your own devices or in your own Google account. Nothing is transmitted to the developer. Camera frames become numbers and are deleted within a second. Mail access is read-only and cannot send, delete or modify anything.

1. Who runs this

Seer is operated by its individual developer, for personal use. It is not a company, not a commercial service, and has no users other than its author. Contact: shyamhfn19@gmail.com.

2. Camera and face data

Seer captures a still frame from the camera every few seconds while watching is switched on. Each frame is converted into a numeric feature vector, compared against the vectors saved during enrolment, and the image file is deleted immediately. No video is recorded and no image is uploaded anywhere.

Enrolled face vectors are stored in a file on the device that created them. A feature vector is one-way — it cannot be converted back into a photograph. Face vectors are never synced between devices and never leave the device.

Watching is off by default and is started manually.

3. Google account data

Seer requests these scopes from the signed-in Google account, and no others:

ScopeWhyAccess
gmail.readonlyCount unread mail and read sender and subject lines aloudRead only — cannot send, delete or modify
calendar.readonlyRead upcoming events as deadlinesRead only
drive.appdataSync between the owner's own phone and laptopA hidden folder only Seer can see — no access to any other file in Drive

Message bodies are not downloaded. Seer requests only header metadata — sender, subject, date — plus the short preview snippet Gmail returns with it. This data is held in memory for the duration of one briefing and is not written to disk.

Google account data is never used for advertising, never sold, never shared, and never used to train any model.

4. Chat message notifications

On Android, Seer uses the system notification listener, with permission granted explicitly by the user in Android settings. On Windows it reads desktop toast notifications through the operating system's notification API. Seer does not sign in to WhatsApp or any messaging service and does not communicate with their servers.

Captured messages are stored on the device for three days and then deleted automatically. Only the sender name, chat name and message text shown in the notification are kept.

5. Syncing between devices

If sync is enabled, captured messages and a small set of preferences are written to the hidden application-data folder of the owner's own Google Drive. This folder is invisible in the Drive interface, is readable by no other application, and is deleted if the user revokes Seer's access. Face vectors are excluded from sync.

6. What is never collected

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising.
  • No location data.
  • No contact list access.
  • No data is sent to the developer or to any third party.

8. Limited Use disclosure

Seer's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

9. Removing your data

  • Face vectors: Settings, then "Forget my face", on each device.
  • Captured messages: turn off WhatsApp reading, or uninstall.
  • Synced data: Settings, then "Reset sync", which deletes the Drive folder.
  • Google access: revoke Seer at myaccount.google.com/permissions. This also erases the hidden Drive folder.
  • Everything: uninstall the application.

10. Children

Seer is not directed at children and is not distributed publicly.
