Privacy policy

Tacit runs entirely on your own devices. There is no Tacit server.

Home Terms of use Biometric policy

Everything Tacit reads stays on your own devices, or in your own Google account. Nothing is transmitted to us. Camera frames become numbers and are deleted within about a second. Mail access is read-only and cannot send, delete or modify anything.

This policy describes what the software actually does today, not what it is planned to do. Where a change is coming, it says so and dates it.

1. Who is responsible

Tacit is published by Shyam, an individual developer in India, who is the data controller for the purposes of the GDPR and the UK GDPR — although in practice almost no processing happens on our side, because almost none of your data ever reaches us.

Contact: shyamhfn19@gmail.com

2. Camera and face data

While watching is switched on, Tacit captures a still frame from the camera every few seconds. Each frame is converted into a numeric feature vector, compared against the vectors saved during enrolment, and the image file is deleted immediately. No video is recorded. No image is uploaded anywhere.

Enrolled face vectors are stored in a file on the device that created them. A feature vector is one-way: it cannot be turned back into a photograph. Face vectors are never synced between devices and never leave the device that made them.

Tacit recognises only the enrolled owner. When it finds a confident match it stops looking — it does not go on to identify anyone else in frame. Other people are counted by the detector so Tacit can stay quiet when you are not alone, and nothing about them is measured or stored.

Watching is off until you turn it on, and turning it on requires written consent recorded inside the application. The retention and destruction schedule is in the biometric policy, which exists as a separate document because Illinois law requires it to.

Tacit requests these scopes from the signed-in Google account, and no others:

Message bodies are not downloaded. Tacit requests header metadata only — sender, subject, date — plus the short preview snippet Gmail returns alongside it. That data is held in memory for the duration of one briefing and is not written to disk.

Google account data is never used for advertising, never sold, never shared, and never used to train any model.

Connecting a Google account is optional. Tacit's presence briefing, local voice, wake word, reminders, weather and on-device reasoning all work without one.

4. Chat message notifications

On Android, Tacit uses the system notification listener, with permission granted explicitly by you in Android settings. On Windows it reads desktop toast notifications through the operating system's notification API. Tacit does not sign in to WhatsApp or any messaging service and never communicates with their servers.

Captured messages are stored on the device for three days and then deleted automatically. Only the sender name, chat name and message text shown in the notification are kept.

5. Syncing between your own devices

If you turn sync on, captured messages and a small set of preferences are written to the hidden application-data folder of your own Google Drive. That folder is invisible in the Drive interface, is readable by no other application, and is deleted if you revoke Tacit's access. Face vectors are excluded from sync and always have been.

Sync is off unless you turn it on.

Planned change: captured message text will stop leaving the device entirely, with sync reduced to preferences only. This page will be updated on the day that ships rather than in advance of it.

6. What is never collected

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising.
  • No location data.
  • No contact list access.
  • No account, no sign-up, and no email address is required to use Tacit.
  • Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party.

Because there is no server and no account, we cannot see how many people use Tacit, which features they use, or whether it crashed. That is a deliberate trade: it makes support harder and the privacy claim checkable.

8. Payment

Purchases are handled by a merchant of record, who is the seller for tax purposes and processes your payment details. We never see your card number. We receive an order record containing your email address and country, and we use it only to issue and support your licence.

Licences are verified offline on your own machine using a signed file. Tacit does not contact any server to check whether your licence is valid, which means it keeps working if we disappear.

9. Limited Use disclosure

Tacit's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR, the UK GDPR and comparable laws you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Tacit's design makes most of these something you exercise directly rather than by asking us:

  • Access and portability: Settings → Data, which shows everything Tacit holds and exports it.
  • Erasure: the same screen deletes any of it. Uninstalling offers to remove all of it.
  • Face data specifically: Settings → "Forget my face", on each device, which also withdraws consent.
  • Synced data: Settings → "Reset sync", which deletes the Drive folder.
  • Google access: revoke Tacit at myaccount.google.com/permissions. That also erases the hidden Drive folder.

For anything we do hold — an order record — write to shyamhfn19@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

11. Store builds are smaller

The build distributed through the Google Play Store omits notification reading and Gmail. Those features exist only in the version downloaded from this site. If you installed from a store, sections 3 and 4 above do not apply to your copy.

12. Children

Tacit is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. Because there is no account and no server, we hold no data about any user, of any age.
